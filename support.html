FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers, Without The Noise.

Once your order has shipped, you will receive a confirmation email containing your tracking number and a direct link to follow your package's journey to your door.

Standard delivery typically takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping options are available at checkout for expedited delivery within 1-2 business days.

Power on your SONORA headphones by holding the power button for 3 seconds until the status indicator flashes. Open your device's Bluetooth settings and select your SONORA model to pair.

Use the provided USB-C cable connected to a certified 5V power adapter. A full charge takes approximately 2 hours and provides up to 40 hours of continuous playback with Active Noise Cancellation enabled.

We offer a 30-day trial period for all purchases. To initiate a return, reach out to our support team with your order number and we will provide a prepaid return shipping label.

If you encounter any technical difficulties, fill out the contact form below with your product serial number and details. Our audio specialists will assist you promptly.
